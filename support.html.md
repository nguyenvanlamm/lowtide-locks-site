Two worlds, one waterline.

## How to play

Tap a stone to walk there. Brass plates at the bottom turn wheels, move lifts and bridges, and raise or lower the water. Two paths join wherever they look joined. At a still stone, level with the water, tap the keeper (or Sink) to go below the waterline, where the undersides of bridges become paths. Reach the bell to open the lock.

Undo and Restart are free. Hint marks where to stand and names the next move.

## Purchases

Chapter 1 is free. The Full Estuary ($3.99, one time) opens chapters 2 and 3. 10 Hints cost $0.99; you also start with 3 and earn more by opening locks in chapters 2 and 3 and by reaching par.

Bought the Full Estuary before, or on another iPhone with the same Apple account? Open *Settings → Restore Purchases*. Hint packs are used up when spent and are not restored.

## Progress

Progress is saved on the iPhone only. It is not synced between devices, and it is removed if you delete the app.
